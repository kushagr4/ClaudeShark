# V2.1 champion review: population-sensitive, not promoted

**Date:** 2026-09-04. **Branch:** `v2.2-development`. **Audit only — no engine
file changed.** Subject: `champions/v2_1_kingpawn` (endgame king-to-pawn
proximity, `CS_EVAL_KING_PAWN`), promoted to V2 champion in an earlier session.

## 1. Why the promotion was re-examined

Two things happened after it.

The **cluster-evidence audit** established that a paired fixed-depth match
between two nearly identical deterministic engines replays the same game with
the colours swapped whenever neither engine's change fires, and such a cluster
contributes a win and a loss to the headline and no information at all. Every
Gate 2 in this repository therefore has to be re-read with an informative-cluster
count beside it.

The **competition-profile pool** became available. V2.1's promotion rested
entirely on `corpus/postmortem/pairs.json`, a hundred positions harvested from
mid-game and endgame phases of earlier arena games. Real Chessathon games start
from curated opening positions around move 6 to 9 with Black to move. A
candidate can be better on one distribution and worse on the other, and until
this session nobody had checked.

## 2. The two results

`champions/v2_1_kingpawn` against `champions/rated_v1`, depth 6, every start
position played once with each colour.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mid-game (`corpus/postmortem/pairs.json`) | 200 | +41 =129 −30 | 52.8% | **+19.1** | 100 | **41 (41%)** | −7 .. +45 |
| competition-profile (60 synthesised opening starts) | 120 | +29 =58 −33 | 48.3% | **−11.6** | 60 | **29 (48%)** | −50 .. +26 |

Neither interval excludes zero, and the point estimates have opposite signs.
Leave-one-cluster-out on the competition-profile pool ranges −17.7 to −5.9, so
the negative result there is not one unlucky position either; the per-cluster
means are 4 at 0.0, 11 at 0.25, 31 at 0.5, 13 at 0.75, 1 at 1.0, a net of
−1.00 cluster-units over 60.

## 3. What is and is not wrong with the original promotion

The original Gate 2 was not a bad measurement. 41 of 100 clusters were
informative — a genuinely higher fraction than the mop-up match's 2 or V2.2a's
1 — the leave-one-cluster-out range was +15.8 to +22.8, and six of the best
clusters have to be removed before the estimate reaches zero. It is a broad
small edge on that population, not an artefact of one or two positions.

What was wrong was the **inference**: a single distribution was treated as
sufficient, and the bootstrap interval that already spanned zero was reported
alongside a promotion rather than as a reason to withhold one.

## 4. Classification: POPULATION-SENSITIVE

Of the four available labels — CLEAR CHAMPION, POPULATION-SENSITIVE,
INCONCLUSIVE, DEMOTED — the evidence fits **POPULATION-SENSITIVE**, with the
honest caveat that both individual results are also consistent with zero.

* It is **not** a clear champion: +7.6 Elo pooled with an interval from −14 to
  +29 is not a demonstrated improvement.
* It is **not** demoted in the sense of shown to be worse: the mid-game result
  is real and positive on its own population.
* For **Daily readiness the competition-profile distribution carries the
  weight**, and there V2.1 is at 48.3%.

**Decision: `champions/v2_1_kingpawn` is not promoted and is not treated as the
V2 champion. The strongest supported build is `rated-v1`.** V2.1 is retained,
frozen and unmodified, as the leading candidate; its Daily package is left
built and hashed but is not recommended for submission on this evidence.

This also means the V3 escalation conditions are **not** met, and no V3 work
begins.

## 5. What would settle it

The synthesised competition-profile pool was built to match the profile of the
five competition start positions known at the time. A public scrape has since
supplied **113 of the organisers' actual starting positions**, which is a
sample of the distribution rather than a model of it
(`corpus/daily/pool/competition_actual_pairs.json`,
`tools/daily/realpool.py`; ClaudeShark's own three rated starts are excluded so
those games remain holdout evidence). A 226-game paired match on those
positions is the decisive test and is the next measurement run.

## 6. Standing methodological consequence

Every future Gate 2 in this repository reports, prominently:

    total clusters
    informative clusters
    informative fraction

and every serious candidate is measured on **both** distributions before any
promotion. A candidate that wins one and loses the other is population-sensitive
and is not a champion.

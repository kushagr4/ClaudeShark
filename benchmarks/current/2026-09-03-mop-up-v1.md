# Mop-up evaluation v1

Date: 2026-09-03. One hypothesis: a narrow mating gradient for bare-king
endings improves conversion of those endings without touching any other
position or costing search speed.

Baseline: `champions/v0_5_2_correctness` (snapshot `6eee6bd9b5e58e29`), which
production still matches (depth-6 nodes 1,712,405; the only source difference
is the dormant king-safety hook). Candidate: `champions/v0_7_mopup` (snapshot
`0b2583b36f705414`): the same files with `CS_EVAL_MOPUP` defaulting on and
weights (30, 15). **Production's default stays off**; this record is the
evidence for whether to change that.

## 3. Activation rule

The term is non-zero **only** when one side is a bare king -- no pawns, no
pieces -- and the other side has at least one rook or queen. Everything else
is zero by construction: pawn endings, K+minor v K (already a rules draw
through `is_material_draw`), K+B+N v K, defenders with a single pawn or piece,
every middlegame. Symmetric by colour; the sign flips with the attacker.

Coverage of the gate suites (`03_domain_coverage.txt`): 0 of 240 root
positions in the competition-like suite, 2 of 138 in the stress suite, 1 of
318 in the self-play suite, 1 of 90 in the conversion regression suite; 5.7%
of positions across the retained fixed-depth games, in 43 of 200 games.

## 4. Components

From the attacker's point of view, in centipawns, added after the phase taper:

    edge      = (3 - min(file, 7-file, rank, 7-rank) of the defending king) * EDGE
    proximity = (7 - Chebyshev distance between the kings)                   * PROXIMITY
    term      = edge + proximity        EDGE = 30, PROXIMITY = 15

Range 0 to 3*30 + 6*15 = 180 cp, deliberately under a rook. No corner term:
the sweep showed edge pressure and king proximity were sufficient. Nothing
else -- no mobility, passers, king safety, material scaling, mate tables.

## 5. Coefficient selection

`tools/mopup/sweep.py`. For each (EDGE, PROXIMITY) a flag-on scratch engine
played both sides from the six real positions (diagnostic, 12 games) and from
24 synthetic in-domain positions drawn with a fixed seed and never inspected
(validation, 48 games), depth 6, 120-ply cap:

| EDGE | PROX | diagnostic mated | plies | repetitions | validation mated | plies | repetitions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | 0/12 | -- | 12 | 12/48 | 17.7 | 28 |
| 10 | 5 | 6/12 | 15.0 | 6 | 28/48 | 17.6 | 12 |
| 20 | 10 | 8/12 | 13.5 | 4 | 34/48 | 17.4 | 6 |
| **30** | **15** | **12/12** | **11.7** | **0** | **36/48** | 17.8 | **4** |
| 40 | 20 | 12/12 | 11.7 | 0 | 34/48 | 17.9 | 6 |

(30, 15) is the smallest pair that finishes every diagnostic game and it is
also best on the validation set; (40, 20) buys nothing. The paired-game result
was not used for selection.

Eight of the 48 validation games end in "insufficient material" after one
ply: the random generator left the attacker's piece where the defending king
could take it, so those four positions (both colours) test nothing and are
excluded from the reading. On the remaining 40, the candidate mates **36**
(baseline 12) and repeats in 4 -- two K+R v K and two K+R+B v K positions,
at 14 and 16 plies. Those residual repetitions are the term's known limit at
depth 6, recorded rather than tuned away.

## 6. Reference / fast equivalence

`cs_mopup.mop_up_reference` is written over `piece_map()` to be obviously
right; `cs_mopup.mop_up` is two bitboard tests and two table reads. They agree
exactly on 2,000+ random legal positions with 0-4 extra pieces of random type
and colour, on every activating configuration, on colour-mirrored positions,
and on every non-activating fixture (`tests/test_mopup.py`).

## 7. Unit tests

13 tests: KQK / KRK / KRNK / KRBK activate; a black attacker gives a negative
term; a defender pawn, a defender minor, attacker-minors-only, insufficient
material, a middlegame, a pawn ending and a rook ending with pawns all give
exactly zero; insufficient material stays a rules draw; edge steps of exactly
2 and 3 x EDGE with the king distance held fixed; moving the defender to the
centre lowers the term; walking the attacking king in adds exactly
5 x PROXIMITY; colour mirroring negates; the flag adds exactly `mop_up()` and
nothing else and leaves a middlegame untouched; the shipped default is off;
the term is under 300 cp at its maximum. All fixtures pass fixture hygiene.
Stalemate is left to the search, which already scores it.

## 8. Gradient

`tools/mopup/gradient.py` (`04_gradient.txt`), white's static with tempo
removed:

| series | baseline spread | mop-up spread |
|---|---|---|
| K+Q v K, defending king on every legal square (n=42) | 87 | **122** |
| K+Q v K, attacking king walking in on a cornered defender | 32 | **107** |
| K+R v K, defending king on every legal square (n=45) | 92 | **126** |
| K+R v K, attacking king walking in | 39 | **113** |

The placement spread grows less than the full 90 cp edge component because
the proximity term also rises when the *defender* walks toward a central
attacking king; the walking-in spread, which is what the attacker controls,
more than triples. Material still dominates: the queen is 936, the whole term
at most 180.

## 9. Runtime

Measured on an idle machine, baseline snapshot against candidate snapshot:

| measure | baseline | candidate |
|---|---|---|
| depth-6 nodes, 24 bench positions | 1,712,405 | 1,712,405 |
| nodes/second | 58,523 | 58,805 |
| tactics | 16/16, 393,472 nodes | 16/16, 390,179 nodes |
| pure `evaluate()` microbenchmark, 200 middlegame positions | 284,214 eval/s | 257,802 eval/s (0.907) |
| activation frequency in play | -- | 5.7% of positions, 43 of 200 games (audit set) |

Search speed is unchanged: the same tree, the same NPS within noise. The
microbenchmark does show the gate's cost when nothing else is running -- a
global read, a call and two bitboard tests on every evaluation, about 9% of
raw evaluator throughput -- which the search does not see because evaluation
is under half of a node's cost and the difference is inside timing noise. If
the term is promoted, inlining the two bitboard tests into `evaluate` before
the call would remove most of that; it was not done here because the
experiment's rule is one change at a time.

and about two to six half-points in 200 games.

## 13. Decision: KEEP FOR CONFIRMATION

The hypothesis was that a narrow mating gradient improves conversion of
clearly won bare-king endings without affecting other positions or search
speed. All three clauses hold:

* the class is fixed -- 12/12 real positions on the bench (0/12 before),
  36/40 unseen synthetic (12/40 before), 18/19 in play (14/16 for the
  baseline in the same games, 17/24 in the audit set);
* nothing else moved -- 0 of 240 root moves, every regression-suite row
  outside the target identical, first-error metrics identical, tactics 16/16;
* runtime is unchanged in search -- identical nodes, NPS within noise.

What is *not* established is a clear Elo gain: +3 with a paired bootstrap of
-0 to +9. Under the gating rule that is not "clearly positive", so the
production default stays **off**, the code ships flag-gated and inert, and
the candidate snapshot `champions/v0_7_mopup` is retained for a
time-controlled arena whenever one is authorised. Gate 3 is **not run** on
this evidence.

## 14. Failure analysis: why fixing the class buys so little

Of the reasons the brief lists, the data pick one: **bare-king endings are
too rare, and the baseline already finishes most of them.** Across 200
audit games the baseline reached 24 such endings and converted 17; across
the 200 gate games it reached 16 and converted 14. The class therefore costs
the baseline two to six half-points per 200 games, and the candidate cannot
recover more than that. The feature does not change move ordering (identical
trees outside the domain), stalemate avoidance is handled by the search (the
one residual repetition, cluster 21, is a K+Q v K where the attacking king
stays away while the queen shuffles -- a gradient too weak in that geometry,
not a stalemate trap), and the conversion problem elsewhere -- the blind
wins, the rook endings, the 48% of errors that persist at depth 10 -- is
untouched by design.

A correct local feature that is too low-impact to move the score on its own
is still a correct feature; the question for the user is whether a
zero-cost, zero-regression fix for an embarrassing draw class earns its place
without an Elo case. This record does not make that call.

    uv run python -m pytest tests\test_mopup.py -q
    uv run python -m tools.mopup.gradient --out corpus\mopup\04_gradient.txt
    uv run python -m tools.mopup.sweep --scratch %TEMP%\cs_mopup --depth 6 --ply-cap 120 --workers 6 --synthetic-each 6 --grid 0:0,10:5,20:10,30:15,40:20 --out corpus\mopup\02_sweep.txt
    uv run python -m tools.conversion.depth --engine champions\v0_5_2_correctness --cases corpus\mopup\bare_king_cases.json --depths 6,7,8,9,10,12 --out corpus\mopup\01_baseline_ladder.txt

    uv run python -m tools.conversion.suite run --suite corpus\conversion_regression_v1.jsonl --engine champions\v0_7_mopup --depth 6

    uv run python -m tools.postmortem.play --cand champions\v0_7_mopup --base champions\v0_5_2_correctness --pairs corpus\postmortem\pairs.json --depth 6 --workers 5 --out corpus\mopup\games\gate2_fixed_depth.jsonl

# V2.4: converting unused clock into depth

**Date:** 2026-09-04. **Branch:** `v2.2-development`.
**Baseline / control:** `champions/v2_1_kingpawn` (`START_FRACTION = 0.45`).
**Candidates:** `champions/v2_1_kingpawn_sf60`, `_sf75`, `_sf90`, produced by
`tools.make_time_variants --module cs_time.py --constant START_FRACTION`, which
refuses to write a variant unless exactly one line of exactly one file differs.

Written **before** any candidate is measured.

## 1. The two measurements that motivate it

**Depth is worth a great deal, and nothing else in the search is.** The
ablation in `benchmarks/current/2026-09-04-search-ablation.md` searched eighty
positions where the mover lost at least 200 centipawns under nine
configurations and scored every chosen move with Stockfish at a million nodes.
Null move was worth −2 centipawns, the aspiration window +19, quiescence SEE
pruning +1, the transposition cutoff on PV nodes +8 — against a ceiling 1,177
away. One extra ply was worth **+647** and two were worth **+899**.

**The engine is not spending the clock it has.** In all three rated games:

| game | our moves | mean spend | clock left at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| round 1 | 29 | 2.67 s | **57.3 s of 120 s** |
| round 2 | 29 | 2.68 s | **57.2 s of 120 s** |
| round 3 | 48 | 2.1–2.3 s | see the rated-game record |

The allocator opens a soft budget of about 4.98 s on a full clock — and then
`should_start_iteration` declines to begin another iterative-deepening pass
once 45% of it has elapsed, so a typical move finishes at about 2.7 s, roughly
55% of what was budgeted. The engine is behaving exactly as written; the
constant is simply set low.

## 2. Why this is not a settled question

`BENCHMARKS.md` records three experiments agreeing that "more search has not
converted into measurable strength in this engine", and one of them is
`START_FRACTION = 0.60` over 400 games at +9 Elo, inconclusive. That result is
not ignored here, but it was measured at **20 s + 0.2 s, about 0.9 s per
move** — and the same document contains an explicit warning that a time-control
mismatch invalidated an earlier A/B, because reductions and null move need
depth before they pay. At 0.9 s per move a change that buys an extra iteration
buys much less than it does at the competition's 120 s + 0.5 s.

So the new evidence justifying a second look is specific: a direct measurement
of what a ply is worth, and three real rated games showing where the clock
went. The prior remains that the effect may be small or zero.

## 3. Pre-registered design

**Family.** `START_FRACTION` ∈ {0.60, 0.75, 0.90} against the shipped 0.45.
Fixed before any result; no other value will be introduced afterwards, and no
other constant will be changed.

**Stage 1 — does it buy depth, and is it safe? (diagnostic, quiet machine.)**
`tools.clocksim` at a 120,000 ms clock measures, per variant: time actually
spent per move, depth reached, and the fraction of the allocation used.
`tools.clockladder` must show, for every variant, a legal move at every clock
from 1 ms to 120 s with no move exceeding its own hard deadline. **A variant
that fails the ladder is eliminated regardless of its depth gain.**

**Selection rule, fixed in advance.** Among the variants that pass the ladder,
select the one with the largest depth gain per unit of clock consumed that
still leaves at least 20% of the base clock unspent over a 40-move game in the
simulation. If no variant gains at least **+0.4 ply** over the baseline at the
120 s clock, the experiment stops at "no variant selected" and nothing is
carried forward.

**Stage 2 — does it win games? (Gate 2, quiet machine.)** An arena at the
competition's own control, **120,000 ms + 500 ms**, selected variant against
`champions/v2_1_kingpawn`, from the balanced start suite with each position
played once with each colour. Reported with W/D/L, score, Elo, the cluster
bootstrap, **and the number of informative clusters**, after this session's
finding that a paired headline can rest on a single one.

**What would make it fail.** A flag or an illegal move at any clock; a depth
gain under +0.4 ply; a game result at or below the baseline; or a simulation
showing the clock exhausted before move 40.

**Cost.** Zero code cost — one constant. The cost is time on the clock, which
is the entire point, and the risk is that time is a resource this engine cannot
convert.

## 4. Explicitly not being done

No search parameter is changed. No extension is added. The evaluator is
untouched and the depth-6 fixed-depth fingerprint is unaffected, because
`START_FRACTION` is consulted only under a clock.

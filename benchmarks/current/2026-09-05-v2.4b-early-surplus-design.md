# V2.4b: spend the surplus early, keep the floor — pre-registered design

**Date:** 2026-09-05 02:05 local, written **before** the V2.4 Stage 2 result
is known (17 of 226 games played at the time of writing; the partial read is
recorded in the results file and is not used here).

## The concern this addresses

Every "spend more" policy that scales the soft budget by a constant also
lowers the clock the engine settles to in a long game: with `START_FRACTION
= 0.45` the equilibrium usable clock is about 9.4 s (spend per move equals
the increment); at 0.60 it is lower. The partial Stage 2 read already shows
the shipped rated-v1 itself dipping under 10 s in 119-ply games. Under a
13-round locked-build Swiss the floor matters more than the mean.

## Candidate

One change to `cs_time.TimeManager.begin`, guarded by a single constant:

```
SURPLUS_CLOCK_MS = 60_000   # above this much clock, plan for fewer moves to go
SURPLUS_MOVES_TO_GO = 16    # instead of DEFAULT_MOVES_TO_GO = 26
```

When `time_left_ms > SURPLUS_CLOCK_MS`, `moves_to_go = SURPLUS_MOVES_TO_GO`
(endgame value unchanged when lower). Below the threshold the allocator is
byte-for-byte the shipped one, so the equilibrium clock, the hard deadline,
the reserve and the panic path are untouched. Effect: early moves get about
60% more soft budget (≈ 7.9 s instead of 4.98 s on a full clock); the surplus
that every rated game left unspent (21–82 s) is used in the first 20–30
moves, where the rated-game errors actually occurred (first serious error at
45–113 s on the clock in every game).

## Decision rule, fixed now

* Run only if Stage 2 (sf60) shows more time converting into score: sf60
  score ≥ 50% with the cluster bootstrap not excluding a positive value. If
  sf60 gains nothing, more time is not converting and V2.4b is not run.
* Gate 0: `tools.clockladder` PASS; `tools.clocksim` at 120 s shows a depth
  gain ≥ +0.4 ply; a synthetic two-regime unit test that the allocator is
  identical to the shipped one at and below 60 s.
* Gate 2: the same 226-game timed arena on the 113 organiser starts against
  `champions/rated_v1`, reported with informative families, bootstrap,
  leave-one-out, colour split and the Swiss risk table.
* Accept only if: zero flags/crashes/illegal moves; the lowest clock held is
  **not lower than rated-v1's own lowest clock in the same match**; score not
  below rated-v1 with the bootstrap not excluding a positive value.

Nothing else is changed. The evaluator, search and fixed-depth fingerprint
are unaffected because the constants are consulted only under a clock.

### Decision

**V2.4b is REJECTED.** Against the rule fixed before any measurement: no
failures (passes); lowest clock not lower than rated-v1's own in the same
match (**fails**, 4.1 s against 6.1 s, and the largest single think doubled
to 18.4 s); score not below rated-v1 (**fails**, 49.3%, leave-one-out
entirely below zero). The expectation stated before the run held: more time,
spent early and with the floor nominally preserved, converted into nothing.

### The time lane is closed

Five experiments, five nulls, on three bases and two clocks:

| experiment | control | search bought | Elo | interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEE (v0.4) | 20 s + 0.2 s | +0.41 ply | +1 | −32 .. +35 |
| time policy B, sf60 on v0.3 | 20 s + 0.2 s | 74% → 96% budget | +9 | −17 .. +34 |
| V2.3 tempo | depth 6 | — | −23 | rejected |
| **V2.4 sf60 on rated-v1** | **120 s + 0.5 s** | +0.50 ply | **+1.5** | −31 .. +32 (70 informative) |
| **V2.4b early16 on rated-v1** | **120 s + 0.5 s** | +0.58 ply | **−4.6** | −40 .. +31 (65 informative) |

The two timed matches were played at the competition's own control on the
organiser's own positions with the informative-family count reported, which
is the strongest instrument this repository has. Half a ply of extra search
is worth nothing to this engine in play, even though the same half-ply
repairs one rated-game error in four in isolation: the errors it repairs
are not the ones that decide games between near-identical engines, and the
ones that decide real games (attack blindness, conversion) are evaluation
errors depth does not reach. **`cs_time.py` stays exactly as shipped in
rated-v1.** No further time-policy experiment should be run without a new
mechanism, not a new constant.

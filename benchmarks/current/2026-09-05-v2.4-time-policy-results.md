# V2.4 time policy: results

**Date:** 2026-09-05. **Design:** pre-registered in
`2026-09-04-v2.4-time-policy.md`. **One change from the design, stated up
front:** the base is `champions/rated_v1`, not `champions/v2_1_kingpawn`,
because the 226-game actual-organiser match rejected V2.1 king-pawn for the
locked build before this experiment started. Variants
`champions/rated_v1_sf{60,75,90}` differ from `rated_v1` by exactly one line
of `cs_time.py` (verified ignoring line endings: `START_FRACTION = 0.45` →
0.60 / 0.75 / 0.90).

## Why this lane, under the new rules

Pondering is impossible (the process is suspended on the opponent's clock),
extra threads are slower, and a Stockfish book is prohibited. The only free
compute left is the clock the engine already holds and does not use: every
rated game ended with 21–82 s unspent, no serious error was made with under
45 s on the clock, and the depth-repair test on the 27 key rated-game
decisions shows depth 7 or 8 repairing 6 of 27. Converting unused clock into
depth costs no evaluator change and no fingerprint change.

## Stage 1 — does it buy depth, and is it safe?

`tools.clocksim --clock 120000 --increment 500` (24 balanced openings, one
cold search each) and `tools.clockladder` (1 ms to 120 s, six positions).
Raw: `corpus/daily/time/stage1_clocksim.txt`, `stage1_clockladder.txt`.

| variant | START_FRACTION | mean spend | budget used | mean depth | gain | ply per extra second | ladder | worst think at 120 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rated_v1 | 0.45 | 3.79 s | 69.4% | 8.54 | — | — | (shipped) | — |
| **sf60** | **0.60** | **5.09 s** | **93.2%** | **9.04** | **+0.50** | **0.38** | **PASS** | 6.7 s (5.6%) |
| sf75 | 0.75 | 6.07 s | **111.7%** | 9.21 | +0.67 | 0.29 | PASS | 10.8 s (9.0%) |
| sf90 | 0.90 | 7.61 s | **140.5%** | 9.58 | +1.04 | 0.27 | PASS | 11.0 s (9.2%) |

**Selection by the pre-registered rule: sf60.** It clears the +0.4 ply
threshold, has the largest depth gain per unit of clock consumed, and is the
only variant whose soft deadline still functions as a deadline (budget used
under 100%). sf75 and sf90 spend past the soft budget on average, which is the
condition the 2026-09-02 experiment rejected on safety; they pass the ladder
but are not carried forward. The "20% of the base clock unspent over 40
moves" criterion is measured in Stage 2 directly, on 226 real games with the
referee's clock trace, rather than on a two-game simulation.

Caveat on Stage 1 timing: the sf60 run overlapped with a one-minute
release-gate smoke game on another core of a twelve-core machine. The
comparison is relative across variants and the effect is at most a few
percent of spend.

## Stage 2 — does it win games, and what does it risk?

`tools.arena --agent champions/rated_v1_sf60 --opponent champions/rated_v1
--games 226 --base-ms 120000 --increment-ms 500 --ply-cap 300 --workers 8
--corpus corpus/daily/pool/competition_actual_suite.jsonl`, started
2026-09-05 01:45 local. Output
`corpus/daily/time/games/sf60_vs_ratedv1_120s.jsonl` with per-side clock
summaries; risk report by `tools.daily.swissrisk`. Results are appended
below when the match completes.

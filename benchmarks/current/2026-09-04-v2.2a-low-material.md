## Decision: KEEP FOR MORE TESTING — not promoted, champion remains V2.1

The term does exactly what it was built to do and there is no evidence that it
makes the engine stronger.

**What it achieves.** Gate 1 passes on every stated target: 421 of 429 audited
false-win evaluations removed, no genuine win pushed under +100, no strategic
win pushed under +100, and in real Gate 2 games the in-family false-win
evaluations fall from 17 of 65 to 0 of 65. Costs are small (+1.8% evaluator
time, -0.6% search NPS) and nothing regressed on the blind-win, conversion,
tactics, 240-position or passed-pawn control suites — because on those suites
it changed no move at all.

**Why it is not promoted.** The Gate 2 requirement was "flat or better overall
**and** target in-family behaviour improves strongly". The overall score is
flat and the in-family *evaluations* improve strongly, but the in-family
*outcomes* do not improve at all, and the instrument cannot see much: 1
informative cluster of 100 in the standard Gate 2, 4 of 83 in the targeted
match. Of those 4, two are genuine regressions where a drawn position was lost
and two are correct losses of objectively lost positions. Zero clusters in
either match improved. Promoting on that evidence would be promoting an
evaluation correction that has never been shown to win a game, over a champion
whose own margin is not yet resolved either.

**What would resolve it.** A targeted match large enough to produce on the
order of 40 informative clusters — roughly 800 family-reaching start positions,
1,600 games — or a Daily result. Neither fits inside this session's remaining
budget alongside the higher-value work, and section 21 of the standing brief is
explicit that a tiny effect should not consume a session in the hunt for
significance.

`champions/v2_2a_low_material` is retained, frozen and unmodified for that
future test. The registry default stays off. The V2 champion remains
`champions/v2_1_kingpawn`.

## 1. Feature definition

`cs_drawish.py`, term `low_material`, stage `post`, flag
`CS_EVAL_LOW_MATERIAL`, registered in `cs_terms.py`. It subtracts a fixed
fraction of the evaluator's own tapered material surplus from the side
holding it, in three exact piece-count families, and nothing else: tables,
tempo, the bishop pair, the king-pawn term and the rest of the evaluation
are untouched. No mate-score logic: the evaluator never sees mate scores;
tactical wins are left to the search, which resolves them and leaves the
family once material comes off.

## 2. Exact families

| family | condition (either colour; the surplus side is the one holding the pieces named first) | kept | removed |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | no pawns, one rook each, exactly one minor on one side, no other pieces | 1/4 of the minor | 3/4 of the minor |
| B | no pawns, one rook v exactly one lone minor, no other pieces | 1/2 of (rook - minor) | 1/2 of (rook - minor) |
| C | one side exactly one minor and no pawns; the other king + one or two pawns; no rooks, no queens, no other minors | 1/4 of (minor - pawns) | 3/4 of (minor - pawns) |

Signatures: KRB-KR, KRN-KR, KR-KB, KR-KN, KB-KP, KN-KP, KB-KPP, KN-KPP, and
their colour swaps.

## 3. Exact exclusions

Zero whenever: any queen; more than one minor on the board; more than two
rooks, or two rooks not one each; pawns present in families A or B;
a pawn on the minor's side in family C; more than two pawns in family C;
rooks present in family C; any extra piece. Near-miss fixtures in
`tests/test_drawish.py` (19 of them: KRB+P v KR, KRN+P v KR, KRR v KR, KRB v
KRR, KR v KB+P, KB+P v K+P, KB v K+3P, KN v K+3P, KQ v KB, KRB v KR with a
queen, KBN v K, KBB v KR, KRB v KRB, KRB v KRN, KR v K, KB v K, KRB v KR
with a weak-side pawn, KN+P v K+P, KB v K+2P with a rook, the initial
position) all return exactly zero, as does their mirror.

## 4. Material-surplus arithmetic

The surplus is computed from the evaluator's own values (`_MG_VALUE`,
`_EG_VALUE`, the numbers folded into the piece-square tables) at the
position's phase (minors 1, rooks 2, queens 4, as in `evaluate`), with
the evaluator's interpolation and truncation toward zero:

    surplus_A = taper(minor_mg, minor_eg)                       KRB v KR at phase 5: 311; KRN v KR: 292
    surplus_B = taper(rook_mg - minor_mg, rook_eg - minor_eg)   KR v KB at phase 3: 202; KR v KN: 219
    surplus_C = taper(minor_mg - n*pawn_mg, minor_eg - n*pawn_eg)  KB v K+P at phase 1: 206; KB v K+2P: 112
    correction = -(removed quarters * surplus) // 4, signed against the surplus side; zero if surplus <= 0

These equal, to the centipawn, the material term the audit's decomposition
reported on the same families (+311, +292, +202, +219, +206, +189, +112,
+96). Family C's guard against a non-positive surplus is exact: the
correction can never hand the minor side an advantage it does not have.

## 5. Reference and fast implementation

`low_material_reference` counts pieces with `board.pieces` and applies the
arithmetic above; `low_material` reads the piece bitboards, popcounts them,
and reaches the same branch. Equality is asserted on 500 random-play
positions, 4,000 random placements drawn from the family and near-miss
shapes, all 983 audit positions and their mirrors, and the 8 fixtures in
both colours. No cache: the term costs +1.8% per evaluation cache-hot and
nothing in search (section 16).

## 19. Gate 2 (`corpus/v2/fw/lowmat/17_gate2.txt`, `18_targeted_gate2.txt`)

200 fixed-depth paired games, `champions/v2_2a_low_material` against
`champions/v2_1_kingpawn`, depth 6, the 100 standard start clusters, each
played with both colours:

```
+38 =125 -37   score 50.2%   Elo +2
naive 95% CI -46 .. +50      paired cluster bootstrap -0 .. +5   (100 clusters)
terminations: threefold_repetition 119, checkmate 75, insufficient_material 6
candidate as White +17 =62 -20 (48.5%);  candidate as Black +20 =63 -16 (52.0%)
```

**That headline is misleading and the reason must be recorded before the
numbers are used.** Grouping the 200 games by start cluster gives a per-cluster
mean score of **exactly 0.5 in 99 of the 100 clusters** and 0.75 in one. Two
near-identical engines playing the same deterministic start position with the
colours swapped produce the same game twice, so a cluster whose pair is
mirror-identical carries no information at all. The 37 wins and 36 losses are
the mirror images of each other. **This match has one informative cluster out
of a hundred**, which is why the bootstrap interval is a fifth of a percent
wide: it is measuring almost nothing, very precisely. For comparison the V2.1
king-pawn Gate 2 had 41 informative clusters out of 100 and the post-mortem
corpus 39.

So the correct statement is not "+2 Elo" but **"at this operating point the
term changes the outcome of one start position in a hundred, and 200 paired
games cannot resolve its sign."**

### The targeted match, and what its four adverse clusters actually are

The 166-game targeted match from 83 retained family positions is the
higher-exposure instrument. It has **4 informative clusters of 83**, and the
candidate scored 0.25 in all four. Taken at face value that is 0 for 4 against
the candidate. Verified against Stockfish at 5,000,000 nodes, it is not:

| cluster | start position | Stockfish | what happened |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5030 | `8/8/7K/5k2/1P3P2/8/4b3/8 b - - 0 60` | **+0, drawn** (WDL 1/998/1) | candidate lost a drawn position — **a real regression** |
| 5074 | `8/4R3/8/5k2/2B5/4K3/8/4r3 w - - 4 69` | **+8, drawn** (WDL 2/997/1) | candidate lost a drawn position — **a real regression** |
| 5041 | `6b1/8/8/4K3/1k3P2/8/6P1/8 w - - 4 59` | **+625 for the pawns** (WDL 1000/0/0) | candidate lost an objectively lost position; the baseline escaped with a draw |
| 5065 | `8/5K2/2p5/1p6/2k2N2/8/8/8 b - - 4 61` | **-534 for the pawns** (WDL 0/0/1000) | candidate lost an objectively lost position; the baseline escaped with a draw |

So the honest count is **two genuine regressions and two correct results**, out
of 83 targeted clusters, against zero genuine improvements. Two of the four
also contradict the family's own premise as written: a lone minor against two
pawns is not always drawn, and in both of these the two pawns win by more than
five hundred centipawns.

The size of the correction in those positions is small — the family C surplus
is a minor minus one or two pawns, so the removed amount is tens of
centipawns, not hundreds. Combined with 99 of 100 clusters being untouched,
the most economical explanation of the handful of changed games is that a small
evaluation perturbation flipped a move-ordering tie-break, not that the added
knowledge decided them. That explanation is consistent with everything else
measured: 0 moves changed on the blind-win suite, 0 on the conversion suite, 0
on the 240-position competition-like suite, and identical results on all 34
passed-pawn error controls.

# V2.2 start: the false-win audit

**Date:** 2026-09-04. **Branch:** `v2.2-development` from `10c9277` (V2.1,
tag `v2.1-daily-candidate`, frozen; `rated-v1` and `main` untouched).
**Audit only: no evaluation change, no feature implemented.** Engines used:
`champions/rated_v1` and `champions/v2_1_kingpawn`; oracle Stockfish 18 at
1M nodes.

**Result: DEFER / SPLIT V2.2.** No single mechanism dominates. The false
wins that survive V2.1 are static (32 of 52 trajectories start wrong at the
evaluation and stay wrong to depth 10; deeper search repairs only 5) and
they come from two different places in the evaluator: the **material term**
paying full price for a piece that cannot convert in a pawnless or nearly
pawnless ending (about 14 trajectories, 27%), and the **endgame pawn table**
paying its advancement credit for a passer that stands blockaded on its
fifth rank or beyond (12 trajectories, 23%, overlapping the opposite-bishop
and rook-ending groups). Each is compact, cheap and exact or near-exact;
neither reaches half the corpus. Proposed: **V2.2a, exact drawn-material
recognition for pawnless and near-pawnless endings, first**; **V2.2b, a
bounded discount of the pawn-table credit for blockaded advanced passers,
second**; run separately, each against its own controls. Nothing was
implemented.

## 11. False-positive risks

* Any rule keyed on "one pawn up" or "passers held by the king" flattens
  a hundred winning trajectories; rejected on the screen.
* Opposite-bishop scaling: 8 control trajectories where the extra pawns
  do win (V2.1 already scores them only +267); a half-scaling of the pawn
  edge would push some of them under +150.
* Blockaded-passer discount: the seven won KB+3P v KB+2P controls at
  +296; and any position where the blockade is temporary (a piece that
  must move). The discount must be bounded by the table credit itself, so
  the worst case is "the pawn is scored as if on its fourth rank", never a
  negative.
* Pawnless material rules (KRB v KR, KR v KB): the theoretical draws have
  practical winning chances against weak defence; scaling to a fraction
  rather than zero keeps the gradient.

## 12. Estimated runtime cost

All exact candidates read material counts and a handful of squares:
material-signature checks are a few popcounts (already computed in the
evaluator); the blockaded-passer rule is the passed-pawn mask (cached in
`cs_passed`) plus one square test per passer; opposite bishops is two
popcounts and a colour test. Each is well under the king-pawn term's +9.7%
per evaluation and would sit near zero NPS cost; a `post`-stage scaling
would add one multiply after the taper.

## 13. Recommended V2.2: SPLIT, V2.2a first

No single mechanism satisfies criterion 1 strongly enough on its own:

| candidate | false-win traj of 52 | control traj reached (of 323) | exact? | cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V2.2a drawn-material recognition, pawnless and near-pawnless (KRB/KRN v KR; KR v B/N; B or N v one or two pawns; K+minor v K; lone minor with a wrong-colour rook pawn) | ~14 | 1 (pawnless, edge <= minor) plus the won fraction of each family (KRB v KR 7 of 40, KRN v KR 11 of 40, KR v KB 0 of 40) | yes: material counts | a few popcounts, `post` stage scale |
| V2.2b blockaded-passer table discount (passer on relative rank >= 5 with an enemy piece or king on the square in front, or the enemy king on the promotion square: pay the table's rank-4 credit instead) | 12 | 21, all won at +970 with the discount bounded by the +87 credit | yes: one square test per passer | passer mask (cached) plus one lookup per passer |
| opposite-bishop scaling | 10 | 8 | no | small |
| one-pawn-up or held-passer scaling | 17 / 11 | 109 / 61 | no | rejected: flattens wins |

V2.2a goes first: its false positives are the *won* members of drawn
families, which a scale factor (not zero) keeps positive, and the synthetic
controls give the exact rates to design against; its reach is the larger
of the two; and it is the only candidate that touches the pawnless class,
which no other term can. V2.2b follows as a separate experiment with its
own record; it also touches the search-inflated rook endings at the
leaves, which V2.2a cannot.

## 14. Exact experiment design, V2.2a

1. `cs_drawn.py`, term `drawn_material`, stage `post`: after the taper,
   when the position matches a recognised family, the score is scaled
   toward zero by a family-specific factor (never to zero; never applied
   to a mate score). Families, all by exact piece counts on both sides
   with no pawns unless stated: KRB v KR and KRN v KR (scale 1/4);
   KR v KB and KR v KN (1/8); K+B or K+N v K with one or two enemy
   pawns and no own pawn (1/4); K+B with only rook pawns of the wrong
   colour v K with the defending king within two of the corner (1/8, the
   only geometric test in the term). Bare K+B v K and K+N v K are already
   rule draws in `is_material_draw`. Reference written out; fast version
   on the piece popcounts the evaluator already has.
2. Gate 0: registry tests, antisymmetry, fast = reference on random
   placements per family, the term is zero outside the families, zero
   with any other material, and a family with the stronger side to move
   scales exactly as with the weaker side to move.
3. Factor family fixed in advance (three sets: light 1/2, medium 1/4,
   strong 1/8 for the piece families) chosen on the synthetic family
   controls and the corpus diagnostic half (trajectories split by hash),
   with the won members of each family as the controls: a factor is out if
   it drops a won position under +100 at depth 6 in more than the won
   fraction the oracle gives.
4. Gate 1: the false-win gallery (21 rows: the pawnless and near-pawnless
   rows must fall under +150 at the root; every other row must not move),
   the calibration set (false wins down, recognised wins flat, blind wins
   untouched: the term is zero with pawns on both sides), blind-win and
   conversion suites, the 240 suite, tactics, `termbench`.
5. Gate 2: 200 fixed-depth paired games against rated V1 and, as the
   diagnostic comparison, against V2.1; retain PGN and JSONL; report the
   false-win frequency in play, conversion and defence, low-material
   conversion, repetition outcomes. Expected effect on the score: small
   and positive at most (the families are rare in play: 7 of 82 corpus
   rows are pawnless); the metric that must move is the false-win
   frequency in low-material endings and the draws held from the weaker
   side.
6. Not in V2.2a: opposite bishops, blockaded passers, rook endings a pawn
   up, anything with pawns on both sides.

# V2.2a pre-implementation audit: KRB v KR and KRN v KR

**Date:** 2026-09-04. **Branch:** `v2.2-development` from `09ba24f`. **Audit
only; nothing implemented.** Engine: `champions/v2_1_kingpawn`; oracle
Stockfish 18 at 1M nodes. Tool: `tools/v2/krminor.py`; report
`corpus/v2/fw/krminor/01_report.{txt,jsonl}`.

**Decision: DEFER V2.2a as a standalone feature.** The hypothesis is
confirmed exactly -- the false confidence is the minor's nominal value,
counted to the centipawn, in a family whose only wins are tactical -- and
the condition is trivial and safe. But the family occurs in 5 of the
1,000 retained self-play games and 3 of the 52 false-win trajectories, so
a feature built on it alone cannot produce a measurable game result and
would be gated on nothing. The condition and the scaler are validated for
reuse: the recommended next step is to run this same audit on the sibling
families (KR v minor, minor v one or two pawns) and, if they pass the same
test, implement one exact low-material term covering every family that
passed.

## 9. False-positive count

Strategic wins pushed under +100 by any scaling: 0 of 0 (there are none).
Tactical wins pushed under +100: 0 of 38 and 0 of 29 at every f. Draws

## 10. Recommended exact condition

    no pawns on the board, exactly one rook each, exactly one minor on one
    side and none on the other (KRB v KR or KRN v KR), no mate score

and nothing else. The condition screen shows what else would and would
not help: "no capture within six plies of Stockfish's line" separates
draws from wins almost perfectly (2 of 90 and 0 of 77 wins), and "no
hanging piece" nearly so (4 of 74, 1 of 65), but neither is needed in the
evaluation because the search resolves those tactics itself -- the
tactical wins reach +690 at quiescence and leave the family when the
material comes off, so a static scaler on the family's material cannot
suppress them. No geometric exception is required or supported by the
data: the defending king's placement (trapped on the rim) marks 1 of 38
and 0 of 29 wins.

## 12. Decision

**DEFER V2.2a as a standalone feature.** Criteria: compact condition, yes;
false positives on genuine wins, none; tactical wins preserved, all;
computationally trivial (four popcounts already in the evaluator), yes;
independently testable, yes; **meaningful number of false-win
trajectories, no** (3 of 52; 5 games in 1,000). A Gate 2 could not
distinguish it from noise and the rule would be promoted on nothing. The
right use of this result is as the template: run `tools/v2/krminor.py`'s
protocol on KR v minor, minor v one or two pawns and K+minor+rook-pawn v K,
and implement one exact low-material term for every family in which the
wins prove tactical and the draws prove nominal, gated as one experiment
with the union of those families' reach. Wrong-colour rook pawn stays a
separate exact-rule experiment as instructed.

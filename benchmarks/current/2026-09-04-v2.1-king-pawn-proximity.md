## 2. Feature definition

One hypothesis: the endgame evaluation lacks relational information about
how each king stands to the remaining pawns, and this drives both blind
wins (the winning king is among the pawns and the static does not credit
it) and false wins (the defending king is among the pawns and holds them).

`cs_kingpawn.py`, registered as `Term("king_pawn", "CS_EVAL_KING_PAWN",
"packed", ...)`:

    distance_white = Chebyshev distance, White king -> nearest pawn of either colour
    distance_black = the same for Black
    eg = KING_PAWN_EG[distance_white] - KING_PAWN_EG[distance_black]   (White's view)
    mg = 0

`KING_PAWN_EG = (0, 48, 36, 24, 12, 0, 0, 0)` by distance 1..7 (the MEDIUM
family, see section 5). Any pawn, deliberately: a defending king that
reaches the attacker's passer gets the same credit as an attacking king
that reaches its own. Own-pawn, enemy-pawn, passer and promotion-square
distances are different hypotheses and are not in the term. No pawns:
zero. Middlegame half zero, so the existing taper phases it in.

## 3. Reference and fast implementation

`king_pawn_reference` is written out over `scan_forward` with a Chebyshev
helper. `king_pawn_packed` uses a precomputed table `_RING[square][d]` of
squares within distance `d`, and for each king scans `d = 1, 2, ...` until
`_RING[king][d] & pawns` is non-zero; `_RING[.][7]` is the whole board so
the scan terminates. No cache: the term costs +9.7% per evaluation
cache-hot and +2.2% search NPS (section 16), which does not justify one.
Equality of fast and reference is asserted on 600 random-play positions,
about 3,000 random king-and-piece placements, every position of the V2
calibration set (550) and of the blind-win suite (68), and their mirrors.

## 4. Semantic tests

`tests/test_kingpawn.py`, 17 tests: A king approach monotonic (a1, b2, c3,
d4 toward a pawn on e5, strictly increasing within four squares); B the
defending king reaching the attacker's pawn gains exactly what an
attacking king gains at the same distance; C attacking king credit; D
mirror negation on real and synthetic positions; E no pawns, zero,
including `3k4/4R3/8/5K2/4B3/8/4r3/8 w`; F the nearest pawn decides, with
the exact table difference; G a farther pawn changes nothing; H own and
enemy pawn give identical geometry; I middlegame half zero and a full
board unchanged with the term on; J enabling the term changes only this
term and no other mirror flips; shipped default off; table monotonic and
zero beyond four squares; fast = reference on random play, random
placements and both retained suites. The registry tests in
`tests/test_terms.py` run for the new term automatically. Every FEN passes
the fixture-hygiene scan.

## 12. Adversarial cases (`corpus/v2/kp/08_adversarial.txt`)

Twelve positions built to make geometry lie. The term moves the static by

0). Both engines err identically where they err (the irrelevant far pawn,
`8/8/8/8/1k6/8/p4K1P/8 w`: both play h2h4 at a loss of 211; the opposition
with Black to move: both e6f6 at 144). The wrong-bishop rook pawn is +548
-> +560, the walled-in king +122 -> +134: the term does not know these
motifs, as designed, and it does not make them worse by more than a
table step. Bounds, not exceptions.

## 22. Failure analysis

Seventh-rank passer episodes (the passed-pawn v1 pathology check,
`15_seventh_rank_audit.txt`): the candidate has more of them (87 to 68)
but they are *better*, not worse: not winning at entry 22 of 87 (25%)
against 23 of 68 (34%), game result for the mover 77.6% against 69.1%,
creating-move loss 138 against 196. The V1 failure mode -- pushing pawns
into positions the engine then plays worse -- does not recur. Where the
candidate lost (30 games): the first-error table shows it erring first in
68 games to V1's 76, and its serious-error rate is lower in every band;
there is no class of position it reaches and then misplays more than the
baseline does. What the term does not do: the deep persisting failures
(section 13) are untouched, the pawnless false wins are untouched by
construction, and the opposite-bishop and wrong-rook-pawn draws are still
called +250.

## 23. Final classification

KEEP FOR DAILY CONFIRMATION. Reasons for: both failure classes improve on
the validation half; nothing that must stay flat moved; the 240 suite,
tactics and cost are clean; Gate 2 is positive on the score and on every
targeted metric; no new pathology. Reasons for caution: the bootstrap
-7..+45 includes zero; the static effect is small and the gain rides on
the search following the gradient, which is exactly the mechanism the
passed-pawn experiment showed can cut both ways; one Daily round is
external confirmation, not proof, and coefficients are not to be touched
after it.

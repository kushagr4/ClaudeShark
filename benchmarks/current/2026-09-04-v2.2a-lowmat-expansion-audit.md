# V2.2a low-material family expansion audit

**Date:** 2026-09-04. **Branch:** `v2.2-development` from `348541e`. **Audit
only; nothing implemented.** Engine: `champions/v2_1_kingpawn` (the Daily
candidate, the control for everything here); oracle Stockfish 18 at 1M
nodes. Tool: `tools/v2/lowmat.py`; report
`corpus/v2/fw/lowmat/01_report.{txt,jsonl,json}`, `02_combined_screen.txt`.
Reference families KRB v KR and KRN v KR from `corpus/v2/fw/krminor/`.

**Decision: IMPLEMENT COMBINED V2.2a.** Eight low-material families share
one mechanism -- the nominal value of a single-unit material surplus counted
almost in full where it rarely converts -- and one safe direction. Across
983 oracle-labelled positions a per-family scaling of that surplus (a
quarter; a half where the rook faces a lone minor) removes 416 of 429
false wins, leaves every one of 112 genuine wins above +100 and all 6
strategic wins untouched, brings the mean drawn root from +150 to 0, and
halves the losses the minor side misreads. Combined reach: 38 of 1,000
retained games, 618 engine moves (1.2% activation), 19 false-win
trajectories in play. That is enough for a 200-game Gate 2 to exercise the
term several times, though not enough to expect an Elo signal; the
promotion metric is the in-play false-win rate in these families and the
draws held, with the score as the no-harm check.

## 4. Genuine-win counts

## 10. Combined-rule candidates

One rule, exact by piece counts, no geometry:

    no queens; the side with the surplus has no pawns; and either
      (i)  R+minor v R, pawnless                  -> surplus x 1/4
      (ii) R v lone minor, pawnless               -> surplus x 1/2
      (iii) lone minor v K + one or two pawns     -> surplus x 1/4

where the surplus is the material term itself (in every family it is the
whole material difference). Included signatures, either colour: KRB-KR,
KRN-KR, KR-KB, KR-KN, KB-KP, KN-KP, KB-KPP, KN-KPP. Excluded: anything with
a queen, a pawn on the strong side, more than two pawns on the weak side,
two minors, two rooks, opposite bishops, and every pawns-on-both-sides
ending. The rule is applied at the `post` stage to the tapered score
(subtract (1 - f) x material term), never to a mate score. Shared
properties across the eight families: the carrier is material (section 6),
the direction is the same, the tactical wins separate at quiescence
(section 5, +690 and above), the factor is a quarter except where strategic
wins were found, and the runtime is four popcounts already computed by the
evaluator.

## 12. Estimated Gate 2 observability

| sample | games reaching a family | false-win trajectories | in-family engine moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 200 (Gate 2) | ~8 | ~4 | ~125 |
| 500 | ~19 | ~10 | ~310 |
| 1,000 | ~38 | ~19 | ~620 |
| a Daily qualification sample (tens of games) | 1-3 | 0-1 | |

An Elo signal is not observable at 200 games: 8 games in-family, most of
them draws either way, bound the effect at well under one point of score.
What is observable at 200 games: the in-play false-win rate inside the
families (19 per 1,000 games at present; the rule removes 97% offline), the
draws held from the weaker side of a rook-versus-minor ending, and the
absence of harm on the other 192 games. A Daily round cannot see this
feature at all; the promotion evidence has to be Gate 1 and the Gate 2
targeted metrics.

## 13. Exact recommendation

IMPLEMENT COMBINED V2.2a. Design:

* Module `cs_drawish.py`, term `low_material`, stage `post`, registered
  in `cs_terms.py`, default off. Reference written out over piece counts;
  fast version on the popcounts. Value: `-(1 - f) x material_term` when
  the family matches and the score is not a mate score, else 0; `f` from
  the three-entry table above.
* Gate 0: registry tests; per-family fixtures (one position per family,
  both colours, the strong side to move and not); zero outside the
  families (a pawn added to the strong side, a second minor, a queen, a
  third weak-side pawn each switch it off); antisymmetry; fast =
  reference on random placements per family and on all 983 audit
  positions; the reference families' 38 + 29 wins and the KR v KN
  strategic wins as fixtures that must stay above +100 at depth 6.
* Gate 1: this audit's 983 positions rerun with the term (false wins
  removed >= 400 of 429, wins under +100 zero, strategic wins intact); the
  false-win gallery (its pawnless rows fall, every other row unchanged);
  the calibration set (blind wins and recognised classes unchanged, since
  the term is zero with pawns on both sides); blind-win and conversion
  suites; the 240 suite; tactics; `termbench` (expected: activation ~1%,
  no NPS change).
* Gate 2: 200 fixed-depth paired games V2.2a (V2.1 + term) against V2.1,
  PGN and JSONL retained. Decision metrics, in order: in-family false-win
  moves and trajectories (must fall), results of the in-family games from
  the weaker side (must not worsen), overall score and bootstrap (must not
  be negative beyond noise), first-error and repetition rates unchanged.
  Classification: keep for confirmation if the targeted metrics move and
  the score is flat or better; revert on any score harm outside noise.
* Factors fixed in advance as rule A; the only selection step is
  confirming the three-entry table against the audit set once the term
  exists, not tuning it.

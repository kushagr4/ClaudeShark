## Reading

* On Stockfish's own UCI_Elo scale (calibrated by Stockfish at 120 s + 1 s
  against CCRL 40/4), RC-C measures about **UCI_Elo 2300 + 96 ≈ 2400**. That
  is a statement about this handicapped opponent, not about Chess.com 2300:
  the limited-strength mode mixes near-perfect moves with deliberately weak
  ones, and RC-C's tactical alertness punishes the weak ones. The label
  "2300" therefore over-states the field; the number that carries
  information is the **large-error rate of our own moves**, measured next.
* The score already meets the 2300 stage's arithmetic (≥ 55%, lower bound
  > 50%) **on the dev set**, which is not the qualification set. The formal
  qualification is a fresh 100 games on `holdout_a`, run only after the error
  audit and the first mechanism-driven candidate, so that the qualification
  also tells us whether the candidate transferred.
* All 29 draws are threefold repetitions (mean 100 plies) — the audit will say
  how many were won positions repeated away (conversion lane).
* Clock: one game reached 3.8 s and one think took 11.8 s at the competition
  clock. Nothing flagged, but this is the lowest floor RC-C has shown; the
  time-policy experiment queued in `spec.md` §7 gets this as a data point.
